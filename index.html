Murad Megjhani is a Post-doctoral Research Scientist at Columbia University Medical Center. His research interests include applying and developing novel machine learning algorithms for neuronal data. His current work focusses on developing sparse coding and deep learning based algorithms for discovering hidden trends in the high frequency physiological data acquired from the bedside monitors at the Neuro Intensive care unit (NICU) at CUMC. His current advisor is Dr. Soojin Park. He received his PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Bio-Image analytics and Brain Machine interface labs at University of Houston under the tutelage of Dr. Badrinath Roysam and Dr. Jose-Luis Contreras Vidal. His research interest include arbor tracing, arbor analytics, EEG analytics and time-series analytics.
